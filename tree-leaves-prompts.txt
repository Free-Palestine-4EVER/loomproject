LOOM — PINK LEAVES FOR THE TREE SECTION
Three Higgsfield prompts. All THREE must match the tree already on the
page: public/img/tree/bloom-tree.webp — glossy stylised 3D, soft
sub-surface glow, blush-to-magenta pink, jewel-like highlights.

Where they go:  src/components/Banners.jsx → TreeBreak()
                src/components/banners.css → .tb-petals / .tb-stage

Right now `.tb-petals` is seven bare CSS <span>s falling on their own
durations. These three assets replace them and add two layers the
section does not have yet.

HARD RULE FOR ALL THREE
-----------------------
TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND. No backdrop, no surface, no cast shadow onto
anything. Each asset is composited over a dark plum page (#0D0716) and
anything opaque behind it shows up as a grey box on the first render.
Ask for a transparent PNG; if the model refuses, take pure flat black
(NOT white — a white halo on a dark page is unfixable) and cut it.

Model: Nano Banana 2 Pro (or Seedream 4) · 2K

LEAF 1 — THE FALLING PETALS           aspect 1:1        2K
replaces: the 7 spans in .tb-petals
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PROMPT
------
A neat grid of nine separate cherry blossom petals arranged in three rows
of three on a fully transparent background, each petal isolated with
clear empty space around it, none of them touching or overlapping. A
sprite sheet of individual petals, evenly spaced, evenly lit.

Every petal is a different one: each caught at a different angle of fall
— one flat and face-on, one curled along its length, one twisting so it
shows its paler underside, one seen almost edge-on, one with a slight
cupping bowl to it. All are the classic notched cherry-blossom shape with
a small split at the wide end and a narrow base.

They are stylised 3D, not photographic: a soft glossy surface with gentle
sub-surface glow so light passes through the thin petal, deep magenta
pink (#F21C8C) blushing out to pale rose (#FFC2E0) toward the notched
edge, faint pearlescent sheen along the raised centre vein, and a small
crisp specular highlight on each. Delicate veining is just visible
running from base to tip. Edges are clean and slightly translucent.

Lit softly from the upper left, warm key, cool rim, the same light the
whole scene sits in. Each petal has only its own self-shading — no cast
shadows, nothing beneath them.

Ultra detailed, crisp edges, high-end stylised 3D render, isolated
cutouts on transparent background, 2K.

NEGATIVE / AVOID
----------------
background, backdrop, surface, table, sky, ground, white background,
black background, coloured background, gradient, cast shadow, drop
shadow, petals touching, petals overlapping, cluster, bouquet, pile,
branch, stem, twig, leaves, foliage, whole flower, five-petal blossom,
hands, vase, water, text, watermark, blurry, motion blur, out of focus,
flat vector, cartoon, line art, watercolour, painterly, photographic
real cherry blossom, brown edges, wilted, torn, red tone, orange tone,
purple tone, washed out

HOW TO USE IT
-------------
Slice the 3×3 into nine 1:1 PNGs → public/img/tree/petal-1..9.webp
Then in banners.css give each .tb-petals span its own
`background-image` by --i, and bump the span count in Banners.jsx from
7 to 9. Keep the existing per-span duration/delay — that is what stops
the fall reading as a loop, and it costs nothing extra.

LEAF 2 — THE BLOSSOM CLUMP            aspect 1:1        2K
new layer: canopy volume, parallaxed behind/over the tree
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PROMPT
------
A single rounded cluster of cherry blossom foliage floating alone on a
fully transparent background — one puffy clump of packed pink blossoms
and small leaves, roughly spherical but irregular and organic, like one
handful torn from the canopy of a blossom tree. No branch, no trunk, no
stem visible: just the mass of flowers.

The clump is built from dozens of small five-petal blossoms in varying
sizes packed tightly together, with a few tiny buds and slim leaves
tucked between them. Depth is real: blossoms at the front are crisp and
bright, blossoms deeper into the mass fall away into soft rose shadow, so
the clump reads as a volume rather than a flat disc. The silhouette is
soft and slightly ragged with a few individual blossoms breaking free at
the edges.

Stylised 3D with a jewel-like quality: glossy petals with sub-surface
scattering so light glows through the thinner ones, bright blush pink
(#FFB8D9) on the lit tops, deep magenta (#D6247E) in the recesses, small
sharp specular highlights scattered across the surface, and a faint inner
luminance in the deepest parts as though the flowers hold a little light.

Lit from the upper left with a warm soft key and a cool rim along the
upper edge. Self-shadowing only — nothing beneath it, no cast shadow.

Ultra detailed, high-end stylised 3D render, clean crisp cutout on
transparent background, 2K.

NEGATIVE / AVOID
----------------
background, backdrop, sky, ground, surface, white background, black
background, gradient, cast shadow, drop shadow, trunk, branch, bough,
stem, roots, whole tree, pot, island, landscape, grass, butterflies,
birds, hands, text, watermark, perfect sphere, hedge, topiary, ball
shape too regular, flat, 2D, vector, cartoon, painterly, photographic
real tree, green foliage, brown leaves, autumn colours, wilted, red

HOW TO USE IT
-------------
Two or three copies at different scales and opacities inside .tb-stage,
one behind .tb-tree and one in front, each on its own slow transform
drift. That is what gives the section depth without a particle system —
same reason the comment in Banners.jsx gives for keeping this to
transforms and opacity only.

LEAF 3 — THE FOREGROUND SPRIG         aspect 16:9       2K
new layer: framing branch at the top corners of the section
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PROMPT
------
A single slender blossoming branch entering from the left edge and
reaching across the frame, floating on a fully transparent background.
The branch is cut off cleanly at the left edge as though it continues out
of frame, and tapers to a fine tip about three quarters of the way
across. Nothing else in the image.

The branch itself is a smooth stylised bough in warm rosewood brown with
a soft sheen and gentle knots, thicker where it enters and thinning
naturally along its length, with two or three small side twigs. Along it
sit clusters of open cherry blossoms and a few closed buds — the flowers
face different directions, some toward the viewer, some turned away, and
a handful of loose petals sit caught on the wood.

Stylised 3D matching a glossy jewel-like blossom tree: petals in bright
blush pink (#FFB8D9) deepening to magenta (#D6247E) at the centres,
sub-surface glow through the thin petals, small crisp specular
highlights, tiny pale gold stamens at each flower's heart.

Lit from the upper left with a warm key and a cool rim light along the
top of the branch. Self-shadowing only — no cast shadow, nothing behind
or beneath it.

Ultra detailed, crisp silhouette, high-end stylised 3D render, isolated
cutout on transparent background, 2K.

NEGATIVE / AVOID
----------------
background, backdrop, sky, clouds, ground, surface, white background,
black background, gradient, cast shadow, drop shadow, whole tree, trunk,
roots, island, landscape, second branch, frame, border, vignette, hands,
birds, butterflies, text, watermark, branch centred in frame, branch
floating with both ends visible, green leaves, autumn colours, bare
branch, dead wood, wilted flowers, photographic real cherry blossom,
flat, 2D, vector, cartoon, painterly, blurry, low detail, red tone,
orange tone, purple tone

HOW TO USE IT
-------------
One copy top-left, one mirrored with scaleX(-1) top-right, both large,
both cropped by .tb-stage's bounds. Give them a very slow, very small
rotate (about 1.5 degrees over ~14s) from a transform-origin out at the
cut edge, so they sway like real branches. Sits above the tree in z, so
petals falling past them read as depth.

NOTES FOR ALL THREE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
· Generate 3–4 of each and pick for the LIGHT DIRECTION first, detail
  second. Every asset here has to agree with bloom-tree.webp, which is
  lit from the upper left. One right-lit clump ruins the composition.
· Save as .webp, and version the filename whenever the bytes change —
  the caches will happily serve the old ones otherwise.
· The section is already on a page carrying two WebGL layers and it
  measured ~6x faster with them off. Keep these as <img> plus transform
  and opacity. Do not turn this into a canvas particle system.
· Whatever you add, run it past prefers-reduced-motion — TreeBreak()
  already drops the petals entirely for reduced-motion users, and the
  new layers must do the same. Chrome's Battery Saver forces that query
  on, so it is a large slice of ordinary laptop visitors.
