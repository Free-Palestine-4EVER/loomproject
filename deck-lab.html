Sixteen cards. Sixteen different objects.

Not one card with sixteen buttons on it. Each of these is a different thing from the world the brand already lives in — a contents page, a yarn swatch, a care label, a ticket stub, a bolt of cloth, a hang tag, a loom. Same four services, same photographs, nothing else shared.

Twenty more, in that lane.

Zine, Stack, Hang tag and Sticker are all one idea: a printed object you could pick up — paper with a real edge, ink off a press, something that peels, hangs, curls or stacks. Never a UI surface with a texture on it. Everything below stays there. If you cannot say what it would feel like between your fingers, it is not in this set.
