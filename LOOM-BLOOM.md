# LOOM Bloom — the typeface, and everything learned making it

**Memory + history file.** Written 8 Aug 2026. If you come back to this in six
months, or a different session picks it up, read this first — it is the record
of what exists, why it is built the way it is, and every wall we walked into.

---

## What exists

**LOOM Bloom** is LOOM's own display typeface. Not licensed, not bought, not a
Google font with a new name: every outline in it is generated by a Python
pipeline that lives in `type/`.

Five cuts, all sharing identical metrics, so the same line of text sets
character-for-character the same in all of them:

| Cut | PostScript name | What it carries |
|---|---|---|
| Regular | `LOOMBloom-Regular` | the plain face |
| Rose | `LOOMBloomRose-Regular` | Art Nouveau roses and poppies |
| Daisy | `LOOMBloomDaisy-Regular` | open blossoms |
| Tulip | `LOOMBloomTulip-Regular` | tulips and buds |
| Ivy | `LOOMBloomIvy-Regular` | vine corners and leaves |

**93 glyphs, 156 codepoints per cut.** A–Z (lowercase types as caps by design),
0–9, full punctuation, three ornament glyphs (❀ U+2740, ✿ U+273F, ❦ U+2766), and
31 accented letters — including **Č Ć Ž Š Đ**, because half the company is in
Sarajevo and the face could not previously set its own address.

Shipped as OTF, TTF and WOFF2 in `public/fonts/loom-bloom/`, given away free at
**`/type`** on the site.

### The face itself

- **Condensed brutal grotesque.** Flat terminals everywhere — no round caps in
  the whole face. Mitred joins. One corner radius (168) for every curved shape.
- Cap height 700 on a 1000 UPM, stem 178, `CONDENSE` 0.86, ascender 900 (raised
  from 800 so accents are not clipped).
- Per-glyph sidebearings, and a class-based kerning feature.

## The pipeline

```
type/
  geom.py       primitives: square-capped bar, ellipse, arc, polystroke (mitred
                joins), rrect / rring (rounded-rectangle rings)
  glyphs.py     every glyph, as a boolean union of those primitives
  place.py      WHERE the flowers go — samples each glyph, distance transform,
                returns the middle of each thick region + its clearance
  svgart.py     turns public-domain SVG ornament into glyph outlines
  floral.py     cuts the ornament out of a letter at the measured spots
  build.py      fontTools: five cuts × OTF/TTF/WOFF2, names, kerning, metrics
  preview.py    contact sheets to SVG (view with `qlmanage -t`)
  zoom.py       a few glyphs, large, for judging one fix
  svg/raw/      the sourced public-domain artwork
  svg/sources.json   provenance: file, licence, author
  out/          build output + caches (anchors.json, art.json)
```

### Rebuilding

```bash
python3 -m pip install skia-pathops brotli svgelements numpy   # once
cd type && python3 build.py            # ~4 min for all five cuts
cp out/LOOMBloom*.{otf,ttf,woff2} ../public/fonts/loom-bloom/
cd ../public/fonts/loom-bloom && rm -f LOOM-Bloom.zip && \
  zip -j LOOM-Bloom.zip LOOMBloom*.otf LOOMBloom*.ttf LOOMBloom*.woff2 LICENCE.txt README.txt
```

Then update the hardcoded `SIZES`, `ZIP_SIZE` and `FACTS` in
`src/components/Typeface.jsx` — they are not measured at runtime.

**Caches.** `out/anchors.json` (flower placement) and `out/art.json` (parsed
SVG art) are the slow parts. Delete them to force a recompute; leave them alone
and the build is minutes faster.

## Where the flowers come from, and where they go

Two separate problems, solved separately.

**The artwork** is real: public-domain and CC0 vector ornament, mostly Art
Nouveau and Victorian, 1899–1913, pulled from Wikimedia Commons. `svgart.py`
flattens each file to one filled path, drops white/background shapes, recentres
and normalises it. Every source with its licence and author is in
`type/svg/sources.json`, and the provenance is stated in the shipped
`LICENCE.txt`. Nothing rights-reserved is in the font.

**The placement is measured, not guessed.** `place.py` samples each glyph into a
coarse mask, runs a distance-to-edge transform, and returns the centre of every
thick part of the letter along with its clearance. `floral.py` then cuts **one**
ornament per spot, scaled to that clearance. A fat bowl gets a big flower, a
narrow bar a small one, and anything too thin to hold one gets none.

That replaced a table of hand-picked fractions of the glyph box, which is why
flowers used to land half off a stem or bunch up inside a counter.

## Traps — every one of these cost real time

**Font drawing**

- **`vertexColors: true` on a geometry with no colour attribute renders black.**
  Cost an hour on the 3D section. `setColorAt` allocates `instanceColor` itself;
  leave the material plain white and flag `instanceColor.needsUpdate`.
- **A ring box shorter than `2 × stem + 60` has no counter left.** It silently
  fills solid. J, ?, & and % all died this way and now carry a lighter local
  stroke or a taller box.
- **Acute joins need `polystroke`, never two overlapping bars.** Two bars meeting
  at an apex leave a white wedge on the inside. A/M/N/V/W/K/Y/Z/1/4/7 are one
  mitred polyline each, run past the glyph box and trimmed by `TRIM`.
- **B/P/R must state their counter box outright** (`_bowl_r`) rather than letting
  `rring` use one stroke width all round — the middle rail is lighter than the
  outer two, and that difference is what buys the counters their height.
- **`background-clip: text` needs an auto-height box.** On the web, an
  absolutely-positioned line must use `left/top/width`, never `inset: 0` — a
  forced height clips the background box the text is cut from, and the word
  vanishes entirely.

**Ornament**

- **Serrated leaf edges read as thistles** at ornament scale, and they made the
  build ten times slower for the privilege.
- **Any vein that crosses another reads as an X.** One midrib only.
- **Concentric bands read as a vinyl record.** A rose spiral has to be eccentric.
- **Lobed leaves dissolve into a cloud** once scaled into a stem. A pointed heart
  survives; five soft lobes do not.

**Process**

- The build got slow enough (minutes) that it must run in the background and be
  polled, not run in the foreground.
- Several Claude sessions write to this repo at once. **Check `git status`
  before `git add`** — two commits here swept in another session's staged work
  (an `engine/` directory, `ios/` changes).

## History — how it got here

Four rounds, each one because the previous version was rejected.

1. **Round one: fat geometric, round terminals.** Capsule strokes with round
   caps, ellipse bowls, procedural roses. Rejected — read as generic.
2. **Round two: heavier and tighter.** Same skeleton, more weight. Rejected — the
   flowers were invisible scribbles at real sizes.
3. **Round three: condensed brutal** (chosen from four directions offered). Flat
   terminals, mitred joins, and the whole curved half rebuilt on a
   rounded-rectangle skeleton — straight sides, one radius, rectangular
   counters. This is the face that stuck. B was the specific glyph that proved
   it: elliptical bowls made it a blob, flat-sided bowls with a stated counter
   box made it a letter.
4. **Round four: real artwork.** The hand-coded flowers were rejected outright.
   Sourced public-domain Art Nouveau ornament, built the SVG→outline converter,
   and replaced the procedural motifs entirely. The measured placement from the
   previous pass carried over unchanged and is what makes it work.

Along the way: v1.100 added the per-glyph spacing, the kerning pass, and the
accents.

## Where it appears on the site

- **`/type`** — the specimen: hero, waterfall, the **poster machine** (type two
  lines, pick a cut/palette/layout, download a real 1600 × 2000 PNG), the
  anatomy cards, the four planted cuts, the character set, downloads, licence.
  `src/components/Typeface.jsx` + `PosterMachine.jsx`.
- **`#typeface` on the home page** — a pinned, scroll-scrubbed act: the word
  assembles letter by letter, blooms through all four planted cuts over a
  drifting petal field, then hands over the copy and the CTAs.
  `src/components/TypeShowcase.jsx`.
- **The nav** carries `TYPEFACE`, which is why `App.jsx` has a `PAGES` route
  table at all.

The planted cuts are heavy (24–68 KB WOFF2 each). They are declared in
`styles.css` with `font-display: swap` and only actually fetched on `/type` and
near the home-page section — nothing else on the site pays for them.

## Open ideas

   nobody gives away a good bilingual display pair.
2. **A weight axis** — Light → Black as a real variable font. Same pipeline, one
   new parameter.

   poster machine as its own product, riso prints of the ornament set.
