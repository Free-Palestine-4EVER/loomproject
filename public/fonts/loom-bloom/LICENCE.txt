ORNAMENT PROVENANCE
  Since v1.200 the flowers cut into the planted cuts are LOOM's own set: twelve
  ornaments vectorised from artwork supplied by the studio, kept in the project
  at type/svg/src/ and type/svg/raw/loom-*.svg. They are used here on LOOM's
  authority as the supplier of that artwork.

  The public-domain and CC0 ornament used up to v1.100 (Art Nouveau and
  Victorian, 1899–1913) is still in the project at type/svg/raw/, with every
  source, licence and author recorded in type/svg/sources.json.
