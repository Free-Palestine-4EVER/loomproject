LOOM — CARD STRIP ASSETS
Two Higgsfield prompts. Concept 04 ("Yarn ball") from /card-lab.html:
the label strip becomes REAL CLOTH, the CTA becomes a REAL PINK SHIRT BUTTON.

Brand refs:  cream #EFE7DA   magenta #F21C8C   card radius 20px
Model:       Nano Banana 2 Pro (or Seedream 4) · 2K
Both assets must be EVENLY LIT and CUT-OUT CLEAN — they are UI chrome,
not photographs. Any baked-in shadow, vignette or wrinkle becomes a
permanent artefact on every one of the eight tiles.

PROMPT
------
Extreme close-up, perfectly flat overhead product photograph of a single
long horizontal strip of natural woven cotton twill tape in warm cream
(#EFE7DA), the kind sewn into the inside collar of a garment. The strip
runs edge to edge and fills the entire frame — the fabric is the only
subject and no background is visible anywhere. Shot dead-on from directly
above at 90 degrees, zero perspective, the weave perfectly horizontal and
parallel to the frame.

The individual warp and weft threads are clearly resolved: a fine, regular
over-under basket weave with soft fibrous texture, tiny stray fibres
catching the light, a faint slubby irregularity in the yarn. Four
hairline coloured threads are woven lengthwise through the tape as
selvedge accents — one rose pink, one soft violet, one pale sky blue, one
warm gold — each only two or three threads wide, running the full length,
sitting flush in the weave rather than printed on top.

Lighting is a large soft overhead diffuser giving completely uniform,
shadowless illumination across the entire width — the left edge, the
centre and the right edge are all identical in brightness. The fabric lies
perfectly flat and taut with no folds, creases, ripples, curling corners
or cast shadows. Neutral white balance, warm cream tone, gentle natural
contrast, no colour cast.

Clean high-end textile catalogue photography, tack sharp corner to corner,
macro detail, 2K.

NEGATIVE / AVOID
----------------
wrinkles, folds, creases, ripples, waves, curled edges, drop shadows,
vignette, gradient lighting, hotspot, uneven exposure, depth of field,
blur, bokeh, perspective, angled view, tilt, background, table, surface,
props, hands, scissors, thread spools, buttons, stitching, hems, seams,
logos, text, letters, numbers, watermark, embroidery, pattern, print,
graphic, frayed ends, holes, stains, grey tone, cool tone, oversaturated

NOTES
-----
· 21:9 so the strip stays sharp when stretched across a wide card and
  still crops sanely at the 2-column mobile breakpoint.
· If the four coloured threads read too strong, re-run with
  "barely visible, extremely subtle" before "coloured threads" — the
  four-yarn rail already sits at the top of the card and two loud
  four-colour elements will fight.
· Want it seamless for tiling? Add: "tileable seamless texture, the left
  and right edges match exactly so the image repeats horizontally
  without a visible join".

PROMPT
------
Extreme macro overhead shot of one single button made entirely of hot pink
wool yarn (#F21C8C), floating alone on a fully transparent background with
no scene, no surface and no backdrop of any kind. Shot from directly above
at 90 degrees, perfectly circular in frame, no perspective distortion,
precisely centred, filling about 80 percent of the frame.

The button is not plastic — it is a crocheted and wound yarn button. Real
fibre from edge to edge: a dense spiral of fine pink wool yarn coiled
tightly from the outer rim inward, every individual ply and twist of the
strand clearly resolved, thousands of tiny fuzzy fibre ends catching the
light and standing up off the surface in a soft halo. The wool is deeply
saturated hot magenta pink, with lighter blush pink where the light hits
the top of each strand and deep rose shadow in the valleys between coils.
A slight dome to the whole button, softly stuffed, so the centre sits
proud of the rim.

Four small holes are worked through the middle in a square, their edges
bound with neat buttonhole stitching, and cream wool yarn (#EFE7DA) is
threaded through them in a raised crossed X, the cream strand visibly
fuzzy and twisted, its cut ends soft and frayed.

Lit by a large soft overhead diffuser with one gentle key from the upper
left, so the fibre halo rims with light. A whisper of self-shadow between
the coils gives the wool depth — no cast shadow onto anything, because
there is nothing beneath it.

Photoreal macro fibre photography, tack sharp, ultra detailed, every
fibre visible, shallow depth cues but everything in focus, isolated
cutout on transparent background, 2K.

NEGATIVE / AVOID
----------------
background, backdrop, surface, table, floor, wall, fabric behind,
white background, coloured background, gradient background, cast shadow,
drop shadow, scene, props, hands, needle, pins, scissors, shirt, garment,
plastic, resin, glossy plastic, smooth surface, ceramic, metal, wood,
flat vector, illustration, cartoon, 3D render look, CGI plastic sheen,
multiple buttons, text, numbers, logo, watermark, blurry, out of focus,
low detail, matted fibres, pale pink, washed out, red tone, orange tone,
purple tone, off-centre, cropped, tilted, angled view, perspective

NOTES
-----
· Ask Higgsfield for a TRANSPARENT PNG. If the model insists on a
  background, take the cleanest one and run remove_background — a fuzzy
  fibre halo cuts badly off a busy backdrop, so if you must have one,
  ask for flat pure white and nothing else.
· Generate 4 and pick the one whose highlight sits UPPER LEFT. The card
  lights from the top-left; a right-lit button reads as pasted on.
· Variant worth trying — the thread carries the CTA and you drop the SVG
  arrow entirely: replace the cream-thread sentence with
  "Cream wool yarn is stitched across the face into a small arrow
  pointing right, the raised stitches forming a clean chevron."
· If you keep an SVG arrow on top, ask for the crossed X low-contrast so
  the arrow stays readable over the fibre.
· The fibre halo needs headroom — keep the button at 80 percent of frame,
  not 95, or the fuzz gets clipped at the edges.
