سُورَةُ الكَهۡف
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
1
الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَنزَلَ عَلَىٰ عَبْدِهِ الْكِتَابَ وَلَمْ يَجْعَل لَّهُ عِوَجًا ۜ ۝١
Alhamdu lillahil-lazee anzala 'alaa 'abdihil-Kitaaba wa lam yaj'al-lahoo 'iwajaa
All praise is for Allah, Who has revealed the Book to His servant, allowing no crookedness in it.
2
قَيِّمًا لِّيُنذِرَ بَأْسًا شَدِيدًا مِّن لَّدُنْهُ وَيُبَشِّرَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ الَّذِينَ يَعْمَلُونَ الصَّالِحَاتِ أَنَّ لَهُمْ أَجْرًا حَسَنًا ۝٢
Qaiyimal-liyunzira ba'san shadeedam-mil-ladunhu wa yubash-shiral-mu'mineenal-lazeena ya'maloonas-saalihaati anna lahum ajran hasanaa
[He has made it] straight, to warn of a severe punishment from Him and to give good news to the believers who do righteous deeds that they will have a good reward.
3
مَاكِثِينَ فِيهِ أَبَدًا ۝٣
Maakiseena feehi abadaa
In which they will remain forever.
