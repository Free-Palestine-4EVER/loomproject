# LOOM HQ — the film

**Campaign:** `loom-hq-film`  ·  **Prompts:** 27 (14 video, 13 image)

> *"LOOM HQ — the studio that never sleeps."*
>
> One continuous night inside the agency. Instead of a team photo, we film the building: a
> converted textile mill strung floor-to-ceiling like a working loom, five storeys, every
> discipline LOOM sells on its own floor, and someone on each of them at three in the morning.
> Fourteen shots, plus the reference art for four new crew members.

## How to run this

Every prompt below is **final and paste-ready** — nothing to fill in. Each was drafted against a
shared film bible, then put through an adversarial QA pass that hunted for the specific ways video
models break. Between 10 and 22 real defects were caught and fixed per prompt. The repeat offenders:

| Failure mode | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Legible text on screens | The take already in `gen-videos/` shipped chat bubbles full of gibberish letterforms. Every screen, phone, poster and document in this pack is specified as *abstract blurred glyph bars and colour blocks, no legible letterforms*. |
| Fabric bleeding onto architecture | The "every surface is real fabric" style block will knit the brickwork if you let it. Shots containing real architecture now split their materials explicitly. |
| Reference art dragging cast into empty shots | Passing `lineup` as `image_references` on a character-free establishing shot makes Seedance composite mascots into the street. Those shots now state the reference governs *material and palette only*. |
| Sung vocals in the score | Music generation adds wordless vocals that read as gibberish speech. Every `Audio:` line is instrumental-only and explicitly excludes vocals, dialogue and voice-over. |
| Stacked camera moves | One named lens, one named move, one stated start and end frame per shot — no crane-plus-orbit-plus-push. |

**The reference art is already uploaded to Higgsfield.** The `medias[].value` UUIDs below are live
and go straight into `generate_video` / `generate_image`:

```
lineup  9ea9b1d2-f15d-43b9-99e2-9eb4b606b32a
spooly  cd43aca0-7b2b-4ef6-9f4d-2757b6c8c115
```

**Order of operations.** Run the four hero portraits first (`IMG-*-HERO`). They become the crew
card art *and* the identity references for the new characters' own shots (S08–S11), so they gate
the second half of the film. Then turnarounds and grids, then the fourteen video shots.

**Cost.** Seedance 2.0 at 1080p/std is 45 credits per 5s block; Nano Banana Pro at 2K is 2 credits
an image. The full pack is roughly 1,300–1,500 credits. The Ultimate plan's unlimited allowance
does not cover either model — checked against the API, both reject `use_unlim` outright.
