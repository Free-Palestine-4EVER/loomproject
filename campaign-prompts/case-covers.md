# LOOM — case-cover thumbnail system (2026-08-11)

One art direction across all 16 work tiles so the mosaic reads as ONE system.
Model: Nano Banana Pro · 1:1 · 2K · quantity 1 · Unlimited toggle ON (web composer only).
Square source because the mosaic crops the same cover into 2:1, 1:1 and 1:2 tiles
(`work-mosaic.css` wm-h/wm-f/wm-t/wm-w/wm-s) — a centred subject survives every crop.

## Shared style spine (identical in every prompt)

> Editorial product still-life photographed on a seamless blush-pink paper set (soft powder
> pink), warm late-afternoon sun raking in low from the left, long soft shadows falling right,
> 85mm lens at f/2.2, shallow depth of field, crisp realistic materials, luminous pastel grade,
> fine film grain. Hand-spun yarn threads in magenta, violet, cobalt blue and honey gold run
> quietly through the scene as a craft signature — one or two loose strands and a small square
> of hand-woven fabric resting near the subject, never on top of it. Subject centred with
> generous empty space on all four sides, nothing touching the frame edge. No text, no letters,
> no numbers, no logos, no readable labels, no watermark, no people.

The yarn palette is lifted from the woven LOOM logo (magenta / cobalt / violet / gold) and the
site ground `--bg: #ffe9f2`.

## Rules learned the hard way

- Covers carry NO client logo or readable type — the real client artwork still lives in the
  case overlay boards. Nano Banana invents watermarks; inspect every corner before shipping.
- Unlimited: quantity must stay at 1, and never more than ~5 jobs in flight (extra submits are
  silently discarded). See the higgsfield-* memories.
- The composer is shared with other Claude sessions — verify the box holds the right subject
  phrase in a call *separate* from the submit click.
